Divine

Overview

Divine is a character created by rudolfgods. The true nature of this entity remains a mystery, but their presence spans both the outside world and the in-game universe.

Occupation

  • Exploring the outside world and the in-game world.
  • Gaining new knowledge.
  • Creating new game worlds and characters.
  • A creative approach to murder.

History & Appearance

The 1990s - The Ritual

The Church of Azazel is a group of cultists from New York gathered late at night in a church to perform a ritual, at the behest of otherworldly forces, with the help of a book that was discovered in the cemetery by one of the cultists. It was decided to hold a ritual by summoning Thanatos (Dr. - Greek. Θάνατος, "death") is the personification of death in ancient Greek mythology.

After some time, everything was prepared for the ritual, all the cultists were gathered, when the deity who was summoned by the cultists came out of the otherworldly portal, looking at everyone, they noticed how the deity looked, it was something between a devil and a man, but it seems their ritual did not go according to plan, almost all the cultists were killed by the deity, perhaps it was angry, and it was disturbed, so it killed almost everyone, and only one of the cultists was able to escape, but this was provided by the deity himself, so that fear and what was like a curse, which the cultist would pass on his message to others, could be sealed in the minds of those people who have programming abilities, and thus the deity will be able to manipulate them into creating ideal living conditions for him there, and he will find himself in the game world of the last cultist in order to start exploring the outside world from there.

1994 - The Game Development

Four years have passed, a group of programmers have been able to bring the game to mind so that everything would function and the like, but the only thing that the Divine lacked was the recollecting factors that would give him the opportunity to interact with the game world, fortunately one of the programmers made a sprite that later decided to postpone as a last resort — the mouse Elianora, this character was an auxiliary, just the one that the deity was missing in the game, after launching the game, the game menu appears on the computer screen, the development team achieved their goal, or rather what controlled them, they managed to create this game, but they long ago forgot that it was not their goal initially, their memory it was partially erased.

1995 - Elianora's Happy Garden

The release of the long-awaited game has come - [Elianora's Happy Garden]. Everyone, without exception, liked the way the character looks, the environment, and most importantly, that the character behaved as if alive, naturally many people playing this game wondered how the developers of this game managed to create such a realistic character, his behavior was almost the same from a real person, with their own feelings or ideas, to which the development team replied, "This is our main secret."

1997 - The Dark Sprite

As the number of purchases of game cartridges grew rapidly, the demand for them did not stop growing, it seems that Elianora found her way to fame, but she forgot about one thing, with whom she shares the game world — players began to notice that there was another sprite in the game, but it was dark in color, and similar to the silhouette of Elianora. Some people began to worry or even try to restart their computer, but the development team assured the players, "this is just a bug, and we will fix it soon!" The Divine didn't even worry about it, because he had already planned the next part of the plan to create his new habitat.

1999 - A New Project

For several years, programmers have been working on a new project, [Sonic the Hedgehog]. The nimble hedgehog occupied a place head and shoulders above Eleanor, and her popularity began to gradually decline in ratings, of course, the deity did not care, it was waiting for the release of a new game.

2001 - Alice's Story

Alice's parents died in a car accident when the girl was 7 years old, she was miraculously unharmed, and she was taken to an orphanage — (Children's Aid Society of New York). As an adult, Alice got a job and was able to earn a place to live, and most importantly, a computer in order to fulfill her dream of creating her own video game and becoming famous all over the world.

She had this dream the moment she got acquainted with the game [Sonic the Hedgehog], and that's when she started inventing her own universe with her own character who would save the world from villains. One sleepless night, when Alice was sitting tirelessly working on a game, and yet, tired in front of her laptop screen, the screen suddenly lit up with light, Alice shuddered falling from her chair when she noticed that the portrait of her character, which she created for the video game, Rudolph the Cat, appeared on the screen.

He quickly explains that he noticed strange things happening in the game, mobs disappeared, and empty sprite windows appeared in their places, and the color of the sky changed to black, or disappeared altogether, sometimes Rudolph felt someone behind him, and it bothered him. Alice did not know what the reason was, but soon she would find out about it, on her own experience, because after a few moments, the same deity materialized behind the cat, which looked like a darkened sprite of Rudolph's Cat.

The main character began to defend himself in order to prolong time, telling Alice that she deleted the game as soon as possible, until an unknown virus [as he thought] spread throughout the game. Alice did everything as Rudolph told her, she started deleting all possible files from the game, but when it came to Rudolph's sprites, she simply did not find them, she was too late.

The work window was automatically filled with text with a message, which for Alice would be the last in the real world — [You will be a great addition to MY game!]. After that, Alice didn't even understand what happened, she blinks sharply, and sees the surrounding apartment change to a spacious landscape of a mystical forest, as her human body changed, that she was wearing a pale red dress, and only then did the realization come to her mind, she got into one of her characters who were previously created for the game, but now SHE is the main character here.

Alice turns around and sees the same Cat Rudolf hovering over her, but his eyes were more anxious, his smile was wide, his pupils slightly oblique, and then it darkens in Alice's eyes, now it was clear where Rudolf's sprites had gone, and the creature stole them to put on his empty flesh, and become one with a game world that now belongs only to him, just like Alice herself, as his "friend".

2003 - The Disappearance

Alice's neighbors haven't heard from the girl for too long, they decided to check if she was okay, but when they entered the apartment, they found that it was empty. The neighbors reported the girl missing, no traces were found at the place of the disappearance, everything was clean and intact in her apartment, even the computer that the investigators launched in the hope of finding at least some clues, video or audio messages did not help, but the video game [The Great Adventure of Rudolf the Cat] remained on the desktop, treacherously exposed. No one else will know what really happened to the lonely girl.

Our Time

After the events of 2003, the Divine drew Alice into a new world, she had no place for peace or logical explanations, the creature was so hungry for new knowledge about new worlds that he made a new vessel for the girl, in which she has been living ever since in this crazy world that follows her.

Physical Description

Divine possesses a striking appearance. Their coat color is brownish-scarlet, making them stand out in their environment. Their eyes are completely black, but their pupils turn white when they become enraged.

  • Height: 1 ' 70 cm
  • Weight: 50 kg.

Abilities

  • Pumping: The Divine has a cursed aura, which makes it impossible to physically stay close to him for a long time, in which case, the victim will feel hallucinations, bloody coughing and seizures of epilepsy. Divine is extremely fast and hardy, he plays the role of "Stalking and attacking mentally" before moving into another phase of his violent abilities.
  • Manipulation and distortion of reality: Only one encounter is enough for the Divine to remember the face of his victim, learn her fears, desires, and then begin to manipulate even when the victim is far from him, which means that the Divine has a very well-developed manipulative system and is intelligent in this.
  • High strength and speed: The Divine can easily catch up with the victim if desired, but in most cases he plays with her before brutally killing her, proving that one cannot run away, fly away, or hide from him.
  • Metamorphosis: Takes various forms, and here is an important detail, he can do this both as an intimidation of the victim, and drive him crazy by taking images of the victim's loved ones, thus the Divine is ruthless and does not respond to pleas.
  • Flight and teleportation: His instincts will always lead to the victim before they overtake.
  • Immortality and regeneration: Even if the victim tries to resist the Divine, he will fail, if he tries to damage his vessel, the Divine will only provoke aggression and a desire to get to the victim before being punished in cruel ways.

Interesting Facts

  • The Divine is ALWAYS smiling, this is due to the fact that his image is literally cursed, oh he must be a cruel tyrant and manipulator who knows no boundaries.
  • The Divine, despite all his cruel image, still knows how to show elegant gestures, but these are extremely rare cases if he likes the victim.
  • The Divine did not kidnap Rudolf's Cat, as in the original Sonic kripipasta. Yeah, he just recreated an image from memory that turned out to be different from what he originally intended.
  • Despite the fact that he dragged Alice into the game just as a given, he still shows curiosity about her, still not fully understanding how she hasn't gone crazy yet (like Alice from the PC port).
  • Alice in the universe of the Divine is not somehow related to Alice from the PC port, although their stories are somewhat similar.
  • He considers himself superior to God and Satan at the same time.
  • The Divine loves raw meat, if he doesn't eat meat, he will be in a bad mood.
  • Divine likes to arrange tea parties with Alice.
  • The Divine really has a heart (as an internal organ).
